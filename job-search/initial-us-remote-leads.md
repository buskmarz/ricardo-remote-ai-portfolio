## Search Filters

Use these filters where available:

- Remote
- United States
- Contract, full-time or part-time
- Posted in past week / past month
- Under 10 applicants
- Entry, associate, junior or mid-level
- Open to international candidates
- No visa sponsorship required / contractor accepted

## Notes

- Do not over-focus on jobs with "Engineer" in the title. Apply if the job description values builders, automations, internal systems, dashboards and product execution.
- Prioritize roles that mention operations, internal platforms, automation, GTM systems, AI tools, customer workflows, dashboards or business process automation.
- Skip roles that require U.S. citizenship, security clearance, formal engineering license or deep enterprise experience.
